# Trade Terms by Channel

> Fictional policy written for the FMCG Sales Copilot demo. Not a real company document.

**Owner:** Customer Development · **Version:** 2.4 · **Applies to:** Egypt, all regions

## 1. Channels
| Channel | Typical outlet | Delivery | Payment terms | Minimum order |
|---------|----------------|----------|---------------|---------------|
| kiosk | Small street kiosk, 1-2 staff | Weekly | Cash on delivery | 2 cases |
| supermarket | Neighbourhood supermarket | Twice weekly | 14 days credit | 5 cases |
| restaurant_cafe | Restaurants, cafes, food courts | Weekly | 7 days credit | 3 cases |
| hypermarket | Large chain hypermarkets | Daily | 45 days credit | 20 cases |

## 2. Volume rebates
Rebates are paid quarterly as a credit note, on net invoiced value.

| Channel | Rebate |
|---------|--------|
| kiosk | None |
| supermarket | 2% above 300 cases per quarter |
| restaurant_cafe | 3% above 150 cases per quarter, on cans and 330ml packs only |
| hypermarket | 4% above 3,000 cases per quarter, plus 1% listing fee for new SKUs |

## 3. Visibility and cooler support
- **Kiosk:** free branded cooler after 8 consecutive weeks of orders above 10 cases per week.
- **Supermarket:** one free cooler; shelf-share target of 40% of the soft drinks category.
- **Restaurant/cafe:** branded menu boards and a cooler if the outlet signs an exclusivity
  agreement (Coca-Cola portfolio only).
- **Hypermarket:** annual joint business plan with a display bonus of up to 1.5% of sales.

## 4. Replenishment and safety stock
To avoid stock-outs, the recommended **safety stock** is the following number of weeks of
forecast demand held on top of the weekly requirement:

| Channel | Safety stock |
|---------|--------------|
| kiosk | 0.5 weeks |
| supermarket | 0.75 weeks |
| restaurant_cafe | 0.5 weeks |
| hypermarket | 1.0 week |

During Ramadan weeks and promotion weeks, add **0.25 weeks** to the safety stock.

## 5. Credit control
Outlets with invoices more than 30 days overdue are moved to cash-on-delivery until the
balance is cleared. Credit limits are reviewed every six months by Finance.
