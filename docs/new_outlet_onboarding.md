# New Outlet Onboarding

> Fictional policy written for the FMCG Sales Copilot demo. Not a real company document.

**Owner:** Customer Development · **Version:** 1.8 · **Applies to:** all new outlets in Egypt

## 1. Steps and timeline
| Step | Who | Deadline |
|------|-----|----------|
| 1. Outlet visit and channel classification | Sales rep | Day 0 |
| 2. Customer registration (trade licence, tax card, ID) | Sales rep + Back office | Day 3 |
| 3. Credit check (supermarket, restaurant_cafe, hypermarket only) | Finance | Day 7 |
| 4. Route assignment and delivery schedule | Area Sales Manager | Day 10 |
| 5. First order and delivery | Sales rep + Distributor | Day 14 |
| 6. Cooler placement (if eligible) | Customer Development | Day 21 |

Kiosks skip the credit check and always pay cash on delivery.

## 2. Starter assortment
A new outlet starts with the **core range** for its channel, then extends after 8 weeks:

| Channel | Core range |
|---------|------------|
| kiosk | Coca-Cola 500ml PET, Coca-Cola 330ml can, Sprite 330ml can, Fanta Orange 330ml can, Dasani 600ml |
| supermarket | The kiosk range plus 1L and 2.25L PET of Coca-Cola, Sprite and Fanta Orange |
| restaurant_cafe | All 330ml cans, Coca-Cola 250ml glass, Dasani 600ml |
| hypermarket | Full range of all 25 SKUs |

## 3. First order guidance
There is no sales history for a new outlet, so the first order is based on the **average of
similar outlets** (same channel and region). Start at **70% of the peer average** and adjust
weekly. Do not run promotions in the first 4 weeks, so the outlet's true baseline is visible.

## 4. First 8 weeks
- The rep visits **weekly** and checks stock, shelf placement and prices.
- After 8 weeks the outlet moves to the normal visit frequency of its channel.
- Outlets that order below the channel minimum for 4 consecutive weeks are reviewed for closure.

## 5. Cooler eligibility
Coolers follow the Trade Terms by Channel document. For kiosks the rule is 8 consecutive weeks of
orders above 10 cases per week, so a new kiosk can receive a cooler no earlier than week 9.
