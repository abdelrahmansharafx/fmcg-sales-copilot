# Promotions Policy

> Fictional policy written for the FMCG Sales Copilot demo. Not a real company document.

**Owner:** Revenue Growth Management (RGM) · **Version:** 3.2 · **Applies to:** all channels in Egypt

## 1. Purpose
Promotions must grow profitable volume. A promotion that only shifts sales from the week before
or after, or that sells below cost, is not allowed.

## 2. Allowed promotion mechanics
Only **temporary price discounts** on a single SKU are allowed. Permitted discount levels are
**10%, 15%, 20% and 25%** off the recommended list price.

| Discount | Approval needed |
|----------|-----------------|
| 10% or 15% | Regional Sales Manager |
| 20% or 25% | Regional Sales Manager + RGM Manager |
| Above 25% | Commercial Director (exceptions only, must show positive margin) |

## 3. Duration and frequency
- A promotion lasts **1 to 3 consecutive weeks**. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.
- There must be **at least 4 weeks** between the end of one promotion and the start of the next
  on the same SKU. This avoids training shoppers to wait for the discount.
- No more than **6 promotions per SKU per year**.
- A promotion can run in all channels or in one channel only.

## 4. Planning and lead time
- Promotion requests must be submitted **at least 3 weeks before the start date**.
- Requests include: SKU, channel, discount, weeks, expected uplift and the stock plan.
- Promotions during **Ramadan** need RGM pre-approval, because baseline demand is already
  higher and the extra uplift is usually smaller.

## 5. Stock requirement
Outlets joining a promotion must hold stock for the full promotion. Sales reps must raise the
order so that the outlet holds **at least 1.5 weeks of expected promotional volume** on day one.
Stock-outs during a promotion are reported to Supply Chain within 24 hours.

## 6. Measuring success
- Uplift is measured against a baseline built from the **8 non-promotional weeks before** the
  promotion, adjusted for seasonality.
- A promotion is "successful" when the incremental gross margin is positive after discount cost.
- Results are reviewed 2 weeks after the promotion ends and fed into the next planning cycle.

## 7. Compliance
Discounts must be passed to the shopper in full. Outlets that do not display the promotional
price lose their eligibility for the next promotion.
