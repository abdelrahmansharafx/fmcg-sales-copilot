# Pricing Guidelines

> Fictional policy written for the FMCG Sales Copilot demo. Not a real company document.

**Owner:** Revenue Growth Management · **Version:** 4.0 · **Applies to:** Egypt

## 1. Principles
1. Prices follow the price-pack architecture: larger packs always cost **less per litre** than
   smaller packs of the same drink.
2. Prices are reviewed **twice a year** (January and July). Increases are exceptional between
   reviews and need Commercial Director approval.
3. Price increases are announced to customers **at least 30 days** before they take effect.
4. Shelf prices are rounded to the nearest **0.5 EGP**.

## 2. Recommended price points
Launch list prices for January 2024, per pack, in EGP:

| Pack | Price |
|------|-------|
| 330ml can | 20 |
| 500ml PET | 17 |
| 1L PET | 28 |
| 2.25L PET | 48 |
| Dasani water 600ml | 8 |
| Dasani water 1.5L | 12 |

Schweppes and Powerade carry a premium of about 2 EGP over the equivalent Coca-Cola pack.

## 3. Price increase history
Due to inflation and input costs, list prices were raised in two steps:

| Effective date | Increase |
|----------------|----------|
| 1 July 2024 | +10% on all SKUs |
| 7 April 2025 | +8% on all SKUs |

Sales reps should expect a short dip in volume (2-3 weeks) after an increase, which recovers
as shoppers adjust.

## 4. Price gaps
- The 2.25L PET must be at least 20% cheaper per litre than the 1L PET of the same drink.
  At launch prices, 1L costs 28 EGP per litre and 2.25L costs about 21.3 EGP per litre (24% less).
- Outlets may set their own shelf price, but the **maximum recommended retail margin is 15%**
  above the outlet's purchase price. Outlets above this are flagged to the Regional Manager.

## 5. Discounts and promotions
Temporary discounts are governed by the Promotions Policy. Reps may **not** offer private
discounts outside that policy. Off-invoice discounts are not allowed; all discounts must be
visible on the invoice.

## 6. Competitor monitoring
Reps record competitor shelf prices monthly for the three leading competitor SKUs per outlet.
RGM uses this to review price gaps in the January and July reviews.
